Workplace Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026. Publisher: Denis Skripchenko, contact: denskrp90@gmail.com.

What Workplace is

Workplace is a mobile app for working in your company's services: messages, tasks, approvals, requests, people, news and files. It connects to the server of the company that gave you your account. You can add several companies, and each company's data is stored and shown separately.

Your work data (messages, tasks, files, HR records) is processed by your company's server. Your company is the controller of that data, not the app's publisher. The publisher does not receive or store the content of your work.

What the app sends to your company's server

  • Sign-in: you sign in through your company's sign-in system in a browser window. The app keeps only device session tokens, in the phone's secure storage (Keychain, Android Keystore).
  • What you do in the app: messages, replies, reactions, approval decisions, tasks, requests, and the files and photos you upload.
  • Device details for your list of sessions: model, OS version and app version.
  • Diagnostics, only when you tap “Send diagnostics”, and only to your company's administrator.

Your company decides how it uses this data and how long it keeps it. Ask your administrator or your company's data protection contact.

What is stored on your phone

The app stores a cache of company data, drafts, unsent messages and settings on the phone, separately for each company. When you sign out of a company, or an administrator ends your session, that company's data on the phone is erased.

Permissions

  • Camera: to read a QR code with your company's address, take photos for a conversation, task or news post, and show video in a call.
  • Photos: to send the photos you pick.
  • Microphone: to speak in company calls.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: to open the app. Your phone's system does the check; the app never receives biometric data.

The app asks for each permission only when you first do something that needs it.

Push notifications

If your company turns notifications on, the publisher's relay delivers them through Apple (APNs) and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging). The relay receives only the delivery address of your device (the push token), your company's server ID and an opaque event number. It gets no text, names or task titles, and it does not keep events after delivery. The app then fetches the notification's text directly from your company's server. The relay runs on Cloudflare.

What the app does not do

  • It shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It contains no third-party analytics and does not sell data.
  • In “View demo” mode it uses no network: the demo data is made up and built into the app.

Reporting and blocking

You can report a message or a person from the menu of a message or of a direct conversation. The report, with a snapshot of the message, goes to your company's messenger administrators, not to the publisher. Your company handles reports under its own rules; we recommend that companies respond within 24 hours. You can also block a person: their messages and your direct conversation are hidden from you only, and you get no notifications from them.

Deleting your account and data

Your Workplace account is created by your company's administrator, who also deletes it. To delete your account and the data linked to it, contact your administrator or your company's data protection contact. To erase a company's data from your phone, sign out of it in the app: More → Sign out of {company}. Deleting the app erases all of its data on the phone.

If you cannot get your data deleted through your company, write to the publisher at denskrp90@gmail.com, and we will help you reach the company.

Children

Workplace is a work app for company employees. It is not intended for children.

Your rights

You may ask which of your data is processed, have it corrected, restrict its processing or have it deleted. Send these requests to your company, as the controller. For questions about the app itself, write to the publisher.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. We will tell you about significant changes in the app.
